I should correct something from my earlier note. I previously said four months was the floor

### What it actually depends on

Not the calendar. Two things:

**First, three developers rather than one.** The work is roughly nine developer-months. One
person cannot compress that; three people working in parallel can. This is a budget decision
rather than an engineering one, and it is the single thing that determines whether the date
holds.

**Second, week one must be spent on safety, not features.** Our website currently has no
automated testing. One developer can work carefully that way. Three or four developers working
on it simultaneously cannot — that is how a three-month plan quietly becomes a five-month plan
with a broken website in the middle. The first week builds that safety net for the whole team.
It is the easiest week to skip and the most expensive one to lose.

already does them:

This removes about three weeks of work without removing anything you asked for. It is also

## What we already have

These are live today. They do not need building again.

**A verified programme database.** Eleven investment-migration programmes across ten countries
— Türkiye citizenship and residency, UAE Golden Residence, Portugal, Greece, Malta, and five
Caribbean nations. Every programme records the same facts in the same structure: minimum
investment by route, holding period, processing time, visa-free countries, Schengen access,
residency requirements, children's age limits, whether parents can be included, tax treatment.

**Comparison tables that cannot contradict each other.** Our tables are generated automatically
from that database rather than typed by hand. It is structurally impossible for one page to say
$400,000 and another $250,000 for the same programme. Most competitors cannot say this.

**A rule that every published number must be sourced.** This is the most important thing we
have and it is unusual. Every figure carries its official source and the date it was verified.
If anyone tries to publish a number without one, the website refuses to update. Programmes not
yet signed off are visible internally but hidden from Google.

Your section 12 says the AI must never invent legal requirements. We already enforce that —
mechanically, before any AI exists. That is normally the hardest part of a brief like yours,
and it is the part we have already solved.

**Bitrix24 is already connected.** Enquiries flow in automatically today, carrying which page
the person was reading, which programme they were viewing, and which language they wrote in.
The system will not show a client "thank you, we have received your enquiry" unless it has
genuinely been saved. No lead can silently disappear.

**Seven languages, including right-to-left.** English, Arabic, Russian, French, Urdu, Turkish
and Chinese — Arabic and Urdu correctly typeset right-to-left, not machine-flipped English.

**A staff dashboard.** Our team already publishes articles and property listings without a
developer, with proper roles and permissions.

**Search-engine foundations.** Every programme has a public page in every language, with the
correct signals for Google and AI search — including the "last verified" date you asked for.

## Three decisions I need from you

Cheap now, expensive later.

**1. Client logins must be completely separate from staff logins.** Our team's accounts control
who can publish to the website. Client accounts must never touch that system.

**2. Client documents cannot be stored the way we store marketing photographs.** Passports and
bank statements need encrypted storage, expiring links, and a record of every access. This is a
legal obligation in most of our markets, not a preference. We operate across Dubai, Istanbul
and Europe, so three data-protection regimes apply, and our legal counsel should agree our
position in week six — before the system is built, not after.

**3. The AI must only repeat what our legal team has verified.** As you said in section 12. It
may explain, compare and personalise. It may never state an investment threshold, a government
fee or an eligibility rule that is not in our own verified database with a source attached.

**Approve three developers and commit to thirteen weeks for the full brief.**

That is a real, deliverable promise, and I would not make it if I did not think we could hold
it. But it rests on the three developers. With one, the same work is roughly seven months
